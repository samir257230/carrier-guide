A few important details

Frequently asked questions

Are the college ratings on this page real?

No. This starter website uses illustrative demo profiles only. Add actual college information after collecting and checking student feedback, and clearly show how each score is calculated.

How will login with email or phone work?

The current page includes a demo sign-in interface only. Real authentication requires a secure provider and a backend. Do not use this static demo to collect passwords or OTPs.

Can students submit anonymous feedback?

You can design a privacy-conscious review policy that allows public anonymity while privately verifying student status. Explain what is collected, who can access it, and how reviews are moderated.

How will the college filters be updated?

In production, profile data should come from a database or admin-managed content system. The current filters operate on a small in-page demo dataset.
